About us
Had a keen interest and involvement in agriculture, advising many farming clients and contractors in Staffordshire and across the Midlands.
We can trace the ancestry of the present firm back to 1730. Today, we mix innovative thinking with traditional prudence and reliability. As a result, you will find we provide practical commercial solutions to the legal problems you face so that together we can form a winning team. We have a team of over 30 Staffordshire solicitors and support staff at your disposal. All staff specialise in particular legal areas and each head of department has many years of experience. We can work with you on a one to one basis or provide an experienced team to work on your case.
